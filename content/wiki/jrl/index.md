Research intern at the CNRS-AIST Joint Robotics Laboratory in Tsukuba, Japan (Aug 2025 -Feb 2026), working on touch for humanoid robots:

- GPU-accelerated tactile sensor simulation in JAX, running in real time
- Tactile-guided reinforcement learning policies for dexterous manipulation
- Hardware & software co-design of tactile matrices for dexterous humanoid teleoperation
- Microcontroller interface PCB reading 300+ analog signals and streaming them over UDP

More details regarding this internship will be made public once the manuscripts are published.
